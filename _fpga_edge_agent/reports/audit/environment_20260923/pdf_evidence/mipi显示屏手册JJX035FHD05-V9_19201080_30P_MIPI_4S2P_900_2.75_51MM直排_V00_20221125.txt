10. Inspection Criteria
10.1. Scope
The incoming inspection standards shall be applied to TFT –LCD Modules(hereinafter called
"Modules") that supplied by Shenzhen Jujixin Technology Co., Ltd.
10.2. Incoming Inspection
The customer shall inspect the modules within twenty calendar days of the delivery date （the
“inspection period）at its own cost. The result of the inspection（acceptance or rejection）shall be recorded
in writing,and a copy of this writing will be promptly sent to the seller, If the results of the inspecting
from buyer does not send to the seller within twenty calendar days of the delivery date. The modules shall
be regards as acceptance.Should the customer fail to notify the seller within the inspection period, the
buyers right to reject the modules. Shall be lapsed and the modules shall be deemed to have been accepted
by the buyer.
10.3 Inspection Sampling Method
10.3.1. Lot size: Quantity per shipment lot per model
10.3.2. Sampling type: Normal inspection, Single sampling
10.3.3. Inspection level: II
10.3.4. Sampling table: GB/T2828.1-2003
10.3.5. Acceptable quality level （AQL）
Major defect: AQL=0.65
Minor defect: AQL=1.00
10.4 Inspection Conditions:
10.4.1 Ambient conditions:
a. Temperature: Room temperature 25±5℃
b. Humidity: (60±10) ％RH
c. Illumination: Single fluorescent lamp non-directive (300 to 700 Lux)
10.4.2 Viewing distance
The distance between the LCD and the inspector’s eyes shall be at least 35~40㎝.
10.4.3 Mura & Light leakage inspection at ND-Filter 5%
10.4.4 Viewing Angle
U/D: 45º/45º, L/R: 45º/45º
